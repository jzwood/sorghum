# Sorghum

#### Print-and-play Edition

A grain-themed, dice-rolling, deck builder for 2-5 players.

## What's included in the PDFs?

- 6 millet field cards
- 18 grain field cards
- 15 windmill cards
- 60 grain/flour cards
- rule book

## Bring your own game components

- 3 white D6s
- 1 black D6
- 40-50 money tokens (pennies work great)

## Printing Instructions

- Double Sided: On (long edge)
- Paper Size: US Letter 8.5 by 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Preview: Scale to Fit
