# Title

Sorghum

# Story

Manage grain fields, windmills, and bakeries to produce bread.

# How to Win

At the end of the game the player with the most victory points wins.

# Setup

### Pieces

- X millet cards
- grain cards
- X windmill cards
- 3 white D6s
- 1 black D6
- pennies (any small token can stand in for money)

### Board

1. Arrange grain cards in 4 rows, ordered by grade, with the windmills shuffled
   and stacked faceup to the side.

```
windmills:      []
grade 4 fields: []             [] :grade 4 unmilled grain
grade 3 fields: [] [] [] []    [] :grade 3 unmilled grain
grade 2 fields: [] [] [] []    [] :grade 2 unmilled grain
grade 1 fields: [] [] [] []    [] :grade 1 unmilled grain
```

2. deal each player a random millet card.

# Rules

Turns consist of 4 phases performed sequentially: Buy, Harvest, Mill,
(Un)Fallow. The Buy phase is only available to the active player but the
Harvest, Mill, and (Un)Fallow phases apply to everyone.

### Buy:

During the buy phase the active player may either purchase 1 grain card or 1
windmill. Grain cards have a flat cost next to their title, e.g. `SPELT ($1)`.
Windmills have a progressive cost: the 1st cost $2, the 2nd cost $4, and the 3rd
cost $8. You may not buy more than 3 windmills.

### Harvest:

The active player rolls all four D6s. All players resolve harvest actions for
their purchased fields. For each non-fallow field, check to see if the condition
on the field matches the 4 dice. If so, gain the money from the bank indicated
on the card. If the field does not already have an unmilled grain card,
half-tuck an unmilled grain card with the same grade behind the field card.

### Mill

If the black die matches any of your purchased windmills, move any 1 unmilled
grain from one of your fields to the corresponding windmill.

### (Un)Fallow:

For each field, if any of the four D6 matches the fallow condition, flip the
field card over -- fallow fields are now active and active fields are turned
fallow.

# End of Game

Once every field has been purchased the game ends. Turn over and sum all flour
cards. The player with the most victory points wins.
