# Title
Sorghum

# Story
In Sorghum you purchase grain fields

# How to Win
At the end of the game the player with the most victory points wins. You gain victory points by milling the grain produced by your fields.

# Setup

### Pieces
X millet cards
X grain cards
X windmill cards
3 white D6s
1 black D6
pennies (any small token can stand in for money)

### Board
1. Arrange grain cards in 4 rows, ordered by grade, with the windmills shuffled and stacked faceup to the side.

grade 4: []             [] :windmills
grade 3: [] [] [] []
grade 2: [] [] [] []
grade 1: [] [] [] []

2. deal each player a random millet card.

# Rules

# End of Game
Once every field has been purchased the game ends. Turn over and sum all flour cards. The player with the most victory points wins.
